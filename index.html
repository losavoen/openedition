(03)Getting aroundRides, boats, a car

Getting around

Traffic is bad during Art Week. Here’s how we’ll get around. Nobody drives after dinner.

Uber and Lyft

For nights out and short hops

What we’ll use at night. An XL fits six. Prices go up near the fair and late at night, so we’ll split rides and leave early.

Rental or Turo

One or two cars for the daytime

Saturday and Sunday have a lot of stops, so one or two cars will cost less than lots of rides. Turo or a rental at MIA both work. I’ll check parking at the house. Parking on Miami Beach costs more this week.

Water taxi

The scenic way to the fair

Free boats go from the Venetian Marina in Miami to Maurice Gibb Park in Miami Beach. Free shuttles go from there to the fair. Last year they ran every day from 10am. I’ll confirm this year’s times.

From the airport

We’ll share an Uber

MIA is about 20 minutes from the house and FLL is about 40–50. We’ll match people landing around the same time and split a ride. Check “Who lands when” below.

Trolley and shuttles

Free, if the timing works

The Miami Beach trolley and fair shuttles go between the fair, South Beach, Mid-Beach and the Design District.

Traffic

What to avoid

The MacArthur and Julia Tuttle causeways get busy in the afternoon. Last year the Venetian bridge also closed in the late afternoon. Go early or after dark.

Who lands when

Fills in from the RSVPs. Share rides from the same airport.

(10)QuestionsBefore you say yes

FAQ’s

Q.
Which airport should I fly into?
A.
Either one. Most of us land around 11 on Friday. MIA is 20 minutes from the house and FLL is 40–50, so we’ll share rides from each airport.
Q.
How are we getting around?
A.
Rides at night, one or two shared cars in the day, and the free water taxi when it works. Tell me in the RSVP if you can drive.
Q.
Can I leave on Sunday?
A.
Yes. Pack on Sunday morning, go to the beach club, and leave for the airport from there.
Q.
What’s happening Monday?
A.
I’m working from Miami and flying out around 9. Work with me or take the day off.
Q.
Do I need a weekend ticket?
A.
No. The $92 day ticket is enough for Friday.
Q.
Which beach club?
A.
Nikki Beach or Joia Beach. We’ll choose once we know numbers and budget.
Q.
What if it’s over my budget?
A.
Tell me in the RSVP. Dinners and the beach club are estimates, and we can change the plan.
Q.
What if something goes wrong?
A.
Call 911 in an emergency. Then call me or message the group chat. The house is at 262 NW 75th St, Miami, FL 33150.
Q.
What’s the weather like?
A.
Warm. Early December is usually in the mid 70s°F (around 25°C) by day and mid 60s°F (around 18°C) at night. Bring a layer for the boat.
