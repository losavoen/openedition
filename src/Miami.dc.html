Getting aroundRides and water taxis

Getting around

Traffic gets bad during Art Week, so we’re not renting a car. We’ll take the free water taxi to the fair and share Ubers everywhere else.

Uber and Lyft

For dinners and nights out

An XL fits six. Prices jump near the fair and late at night, so we’ll split rides and leave a little early.

Water taxi

How we get to the fair

Free boats every 7–9 minutes from the Venetian Marina (1635 N Bayshore Dr) to Maurice Gibb Park (1790 Purdy Ave) in Miami Beach. A free shuttle runs from there to the Convention Center. Daily from 10am to midnight, and until 10pm on Sunday.

From the airport

Share a ride

MIA is about 20 minutes from the house and FLL is 40 to 50. If you land around the same time as someone else, split an Uber. You can see who lands when below.

Trolley and shuttles

Free, if the timing works

The Miami Beach trolley and fair shuttles go between the fair, South Beach, Mid-Beach and the Design District.

Traffic

What to avoid

The MacArthur and Julia Tuttle causeways get busy in the afternoon. Last year the Venetian bridge also closed in the late afternoon. Go early or after dark.

QuestionsBefore you say yes

Questions

Q.
Which airport should I fly into?
A.
Either works. MIA is 20 minutes from the house and FLL is 40 to 50. If you land around the same time as someone else, share a ride.
Q.
How are we getting around?
A.
We’re not renting a car. We’ll take the free water taxi to the fair and share Ubers everywhere else.
Q.
Can I leave on Sunday?
A.
Yes. Pack in the morning, come to Casa Neos at 3:30, and head to the airport from there.
Q.
What’s happening Monday?
A.
I’m working from Miami and flying out around 9pm. Work with me, take the day off, or join the few of us going to Nikki Beach.
Q.
Do I need a weekend ticket?
A.
No. We’re only getting the $92 day ticket for Saturday.
Q.
Is there a beach club?
A.
Yes, Nikki Beach on Monday. Only a few of us are going, so join if you’re staying and it fits your budget.
Q.
What if it’s over my budget?
A.
Tell me in the RSVP. The dinner prices are estimates and we can adjust the plan.
Q.
What if something goes wrong?
A.
Call 911 in an emergency. Then call me or message the group chat. The house address is in “Where we’re staying”.
Q.
What’s the weather like?
A.
Warm. Early December is usually in the mid 70s°F (around 25°C) by day and mid 60s°F (around 18°C) at night. Bring a layer for the evenings.
